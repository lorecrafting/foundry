# Portable custody: revoked-issuer reconciliation

[Foundry](../../README.md) › [Docs](../README.md) › Portable custody reconciliation

**Status:** proposed design-only prerequisite for [ML-PG-CUSTODY](../REPAIR-PLAN.md#portable-milestone-tickets-in-dependency-order); requires independent review and explicit operator policy decisions before any protected Core change. Not an installed service, a receipt, or acceptance of the parent ticket. Source inspected at main `7a41ada9e6a589af17845b0c41b146c92e44424c`; the unmerged custody candidate is **`bb8617e02cfcd13e2e6bfe5f935d952132e0207c`** (independent correction reviews: `/private/tmp/ML-PG-CUSTODY.review-{4,5}.md`).

## Boundary and failure

The first-host [custody design](PORTABLE-CUSTODY.md) permanently binds UID to principal/role, refuses revoked callers even for cached results, and leaves their outstanding work for an operator. The [portable contract](PORTABLE-GOVERNANCE-CONTRACT.md) requires attributable receipts and pending/unknown outcomes. The manual lane is a **separate, human-dispatched** starting route, not evidence of installed peer authentication. In main, `lib/foundry/durable_store/protected/operations.ex` `settlement_provenance/3` requires the `settle_claim` authenticated actor to equal the effect issuer and the request to equal the effect request; `first_settlement/5` holds reservations and leases for `unknown`, while known outcomes settle them. `lib/foundry/manual_lane/backend.ex` `current_effect/4` also requires the issuer; `settle_root/6` writes that actor's receipt. These ordinary receipts must keep their provenance. In the unmerged candidate, `lib/foundry/portable/custody.ex` `access/5` denies operator `settle`, `bound_action/8` pins submit/verdict/settle to the original attempt, effect and claim, and `outstanding/4` now exposes succeeded-but-unclosed executions as well as issued/unknown claims. `recover` restores the store owner, not a claim or review. The two independent reviews (`/private/tmp/ML-PG-CUSTODY.review-{4,5}.md`) find revoked work cannot be reconciled through that route.

## Minimum protected proposal (not blanket authorization for Core)

Introduce a **separate operator reconciliation command**, not an operator-shaped `settle_claim` or a synthetic issuer receipt. The installed service must authenticate a currently enrolled operator peer through the protected endpoint, prohibit server-side paths and self-declared actor/role, and write a distinct durable operator decision with operator principal, request ID, evidence references/digest, reason, original issuer, ticket/attempt/execution/effect/claim IDs and observed revisions. Core, not the caller or Backend's active-attempt pointer, must verify the original issued claim, its immutable owner/role/request/epoch and associated reservation/lease; the execution must belong to the named attempt and ticket. Compare-and-set those facts and exclude another settlement, successor launch or conflicting recovery across restart. Exact retry returns the same recorded decision to an authorized operator; changed content or another actor refuses. Keep the original receipt(s) and issuer attribution untouched; the operator record is evidence *about reconciliation*, never a claim that the issuer reported anything. Do not enable this command via generic root writes or reuse `settle_claim` by relaxing `authenticated_actor == issuer`.

| Evidence and decision | Protected effect and parent handling |
|---|---|
| Already has an attributable issuer success/failure receipt | Preserve it; do not issue another receipt or re-settle the claim. A separately authenticated operator may request only the **original** pinned workflow closure if all its required delivery, stream/seal, verdict and termination facts exist. A delivered receipt alone is not closure or approval. If any fact is missing, keep the execution pending and report the missing fact; never manufacture a human review verdict. |
| Proved no start/delivery | Require independent, durable proof that the original issuer **and channel** cannot still act, plus attributable non-delivery of this exact operation (not merely expiry, revocation, missing pane or an operator assertion). Only after Core verifies the proof may a distinct operator disposition mark this claim `non_started`, settle its original reservation once, release only proved-safe leases, close that execution and apply the correct parent role/attempt R4a row. Retain predecessor identity and bounded ordinal before any successor. |
| Started/possibly delivered, but outcome unproved | Record `unknown` against the original claim without pretending an issuer receipt; retain holds, conflict/checkout leases, execution slot and original attempt; no successor or final closure. Further evidence may resolve only that same claim. |
| Conflicting receipts, unverified evidence, moved parent, absent/corrupt fact or unsafe checkout | Refuse a terminal decision; quarantine/keep unknown and exclusions, surface exact IDs and conflict to the operator. A closed execution with `reconciliation_required` is not cleared by the ordinary receipt path. |

The existing Core `unknown`-to-known `reconciled_settlement/5` is issuer-provenance-gated; a new operator path must independently validate proof and perform one atomic transition with the original ledger/lease and parent facts, or refuse. No effect-only fix may declare the attempt terminal: `Backend.review/8` separates delivery from seal/verdict/closure, and R4a requires role-specific attempt state and a predecessor before retry. The protected verifier owns claim, ledger and receipt provenance; the manual-lane lifecycle still owns its phase/closure, so integration needs an explicit reviewed cross-boundary rule rather than pretending a Core outcome alone advances it. [R1](../WORKFLOW-CONTRACT.md#durable-claims-and-recovery--r1), [R3](../WORKFLOW-CONTRACT.md#r3-autonomously-repairable-kernel-protected-verifier) and [R4a](../WORKFLOW-CONTRACT.md#launch-non-start-recovery--r4a) forbid observation-as-authority and timeout-as-non-start. This narrow proposal does not authorize every R4a/FR-10 outcome or general autonomous recovery.

**Packet-result cut is a separate blocker.** The unmerged `Custody.ordinary_execute/8` stores an intent before `Backend.launch/4`; `bound_action/8` checks a predicted original execution but, after its claim is consumed or the attempt moves, returns `custody_unavailable` with the intent pending (`result` returns `request_pending`). `Backend.packet/3` calls `WorkPacket.build/3`, which requires an **active attempt and issued claim** (`lib/foundry/work_packet.ex`); current facts do not preserve the original packet bytes. No exact original packet can be safely reconstructed from a consumed claim or a later active attempt. Do not replay `launch`, retarget the intent, present the later packet as its result, or return success on the strength of a matching effect ID. Keep it pending and prevent successor issuance where the original remains unresolved. A separate reviewed design must identify a pre-consumption durable packet snapshot/result (bound to request digest, actor, original effect/claim and policy/spec revisions), or a proved safe terminal refusal semantics; without such facts this cut remains blocked, not covered by the operator claim command.

## Acceptance to authorize implementation later

- **Positive/restart:** real protected operator peer reconciles a revoked issued claim with proved quiescence **and** non-delivery; Core and original attempt/execution, infrastructure ordinal, reservation and leases agree before and after reopen. A later retry names its terminal predecessor, never a new owner for the original. An issuer's ordinary receipt remains issuer-authored; an operator decision remains operator-authored.
- **Distinct cuts:** revoke before issue (no productive issue); issue then revoke/possibly deliver (unknown with holds); issuer succeeds then reply is lost (exact receipt retained, no duplicate); delivery succeeds before review seal/verdict/close (still pending); complete original review then lose closure response (only pinned close resumes); conflicting/late receipt (quarantine). Test developer and reviewer parent rows independently; never approve from delivery alone. Close/reopen at each committed cut and verify Gateway ready and no new effect.
- **Red controls/refusals:** remove the new proof/claim/attempt CAS guard in scratch and show a test fail for each distinct bypass. Operator `settle_claim` impersonation, revoked UID on old connection/restart, sibling/wrong project, reused claim/request ID, stale evidence, expiry-only or issuer-exit-only non-start, moved attempt, changed result content, and absent packet snapshot all refuse or remain explicitly unknown. A positive already-issued packet exact retry works only if its result is durably bound; the consumed-claim cut remains pending until its separate design is reviewed.

**Human decisions needed:** which installed evidence sources can *prove non-delivery and channel quiescence* per operation (local process exit alone may not prove remote non-delivery); whether the first narrow implementation supports non-start at all or only records unknown; and who may attest externally established success without a surviving issuer receipt. None is silently decided here. Host provisioning, distinct-UID hostile-tool tests, trusted human review-only submission, and macOS/Linux restart checks remain the parent ML-PG-CUSTODY obligations. Candidate import, trusted checks and accepted-ref promotion remain their later ticket owners. No service, Core change, test or deployment is included in this proposal.
